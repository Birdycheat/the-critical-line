Decompiling the formal proof for human readers. The formalization published by Levent Alpöge, working with Claude, proves that every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function and of every Dirichlet L-function lies on the critical line σ = ½. Because all 175,187 modules have already been verified by independent proof kernels, the central scientific task is conceptual: reconstructing the human argument, checking exact definitions and hypotheses against the pinned source, and simplifying machine-generated chains.

Methodology, deterministic guardrails & open human review

Directed and edited by Andréa Gourion under CC BY-NC 4.0, this edition combines frontier agentic reconstruction directly from the pinned formal archive and its 61.5 GB uncompressed proof export with deterministic verification scripts that check every pinned line range, acyclic dependency edge, exported constant reference, and all 92 numerical experiments.

Remaining encapsulated frontiers & human audit: Major upstream blocks still cited as verified formal interfaces include symplectic/unitary deformation rings in the Plus bound, the regularized Eisenstein/Siegel–Weil input, orbital period expansions, and arithmetic prime-window instantiation.
