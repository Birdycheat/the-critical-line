1Glossary

Analytic continuation
An extension agreeing with a given analytic function on its original domain. Agreement on an open subset determines the continuation on a connected domain. Read in context
Annihilator
The characters of a finite abelian group that are identically one on a specified subgroup. Subgroup averaging selects exactly these characters. Read in context
Automorphic function
A function on an adelic group that descends under its rational subgroup and satisfies the prescribed regularity and other conditions. Square-integrability on the quotient matters for the lifts here. Read in context
Character / χ
A homomorphism from a finite group to nonzero complex numbers. A Dirichlet character extends by zero off the units modulo N. Read in context
Conductor / f
The least divisor of the modulus from which a Dirichlet character is induced. A primitive character has conductor equal to its modulus. Read in context
Covolume
The volume of a fundamental parallelepiped of a lattice in its real span with the chosen metric. Read in context
Critical strip / σ
The region 0 < Re s < 1. Here s = σ+it; σ = ½ is the critical line. Read in context
Euler’s constant / γ
The limit of 1+1/2+⋯+1/n − log n; it is the constant Laurent coefficient of ζ at 1. Read in context
Formal group and formal logarithm
A group law in power series at the identity and a change of coordinate taking that law to addition. Read in context
Frobenius and inertia
Inertia is the subgroup of a local Galois group acting trivially on the residue field. A Frobenius class acts there by raising to the residue-field size. Read in context
Mellin transform
Here the integral from 1 to infinity against x^(−s−1) dx. Under x=eᵘ it becomes a Laplace transform. Read in context
Multiplicity / m
At an isolated zero ρ, the integer m≥1 such that L(s)=(s−ρ)ᵐh(s) with h holomorphic and h(ρ)≠0. The balance chapter also uses m for a chain length. Read in context
Pole and residue
The residue is the coefficient of (s−ρ)⁻¹ in a Laurent expansion. A simple pole has a nonzero residue and no higher-order singular terms. Read in context
Positive part / u₊
The number max(u,0). In a ramp it cuts off the sum; in the window estimate it keeps the side controlled by the hypothesis. Read in context
Ramification index
The multiplicity of a prime in the extended prime ideal. It enters the weighting of the finite-place contributions to divisibility. Read in context
Sha-type local kernel
The subgroup of degree-one Galois cohomology classes whose restrictions at the specified places vanish. Its module length enters the cohomological window bound. Read in context
Slope
For a Euclidean lattice of positive rank, its Arakelov slope is the negative log of its covolume divided by its rank. Extremal slopes measure sublattices and quotients. Read in context
Tate twist
A change in a Galois action by a power of the cyclotomic character, which records the action on roots of unity. Its exponent matters in a local restriction kernel. Read in context
Theta lift
An integral operator with a theta kernel carrying an automorphic function between spaces for two groups. Read in context
Valuation / vₚ
The exponent of p in a nonzero rational number, extended by vₚ(0)=+∞. The normalized p-adic absolute value is p to the power −vₚ. Read in context
Von Mangoldt function / Λ
Λ(pʲ)=log p for a prime p and integer j≥1; its value at every other positive integer is zero. Read in context
Window / Y, U, δ
An interval [Y,Y¹⁺ᵟ] with δ>0; in logarithmic coordinates its endpoints are U=log Y and (1+δ)U. Read in context

3The critical strip, in section

The frontispiece plots |ζ(σ+it)| on 27 equally spaced sections 0≤σ≤1. Each section contains 169 samples, 8≤t≤32. The copper section on this page has σ=½. The opening figure allows other sampled sections to be selected. Height is linear in |ζ|. Smoothly joined sample values can miss a zero between mesh points.

The generator sums the first 95 Dirichlet terms, then applies Euler–Maclaurin at N=96 through B₆. The absolute remainder is bounded by |s(s+1)⋯(s+5)| N−σ−5/[30240(σ+5)] from the periodic Bernoulli remainder. This is less than 0.000002 across the plotted domain.
