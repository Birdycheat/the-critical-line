1Glossary

Adeles
A compatible list of local entries, one in each completion of a number field, integral at all but finitely many finite places. The diagonal embedding sends one field element to its value in every completion. Read in context
Analytic continuation
An extension agreeing with a given analytic function on its original domain. Agreement on an open subset determines the continuation on a connected domain. Read in context
Annihilator
The characters of a finite abelian group that are identically one on a specified subgroup. Subgroup averaging selects exactly these characters. Read in context
Automorphic function
A function on an adelic group that descends under its rational subgroup and satisfies the prescribed regularity and other conditions. Square-integrability on the quotient matters for the lifts here. Read in context
Bar chain
A finite linear combination of ordered words in a group. Its boundary takes alternating faces obtained by omitting an end entry or multiplying neighboring entries. Read in context
Bernoulli value of a character
A weighted finite sum of Bernoulli polynomial values at fractions of the conductor. The minus estimate clears its denominators and uses the same algebraic integer at every coefficient prime. Read in context
Bockstein map
In the coefficient sequence for reduction modulo q, lift a cycle to an integral chain. Its boundary is a multiple of q. Dividing that boundary by q gives a class one degree lower, killed by q. This map distinguishes the two torsion orders in the cycle count. Read in context
Character / χ
A homomorphism from a finite group to nonzero complex numbers. A Dirichlet character extends by zero off the units modulo N. Read in context
Class group / Cl(K)
Fractional ideals of the number field K modulo principal fractional ideals. The quotient Cl(K)/ℓCl(K) measures the number of independent characters of order ℓ. Read in context
CM field
A number field that is a totally imaginary quadratic extension of a totally real field. Complex conjugation interchanges the two embeddings over any real place of the base. Read in context
Cocycle
A function c satisfying c(gh)=c(g)+g·c(h). Its class in degree one identifies functions differing by g↦g·a−a. Local conditions impose restrictions on these classes. Read in context
Cocycle test on a prism
A linear functional that vanishes on boundaries. For the residue test, consecutive pairs contribute carries and the last entry contributes an additive index. Read in context
Conductor / f
The least divisor of the modulus from which a Dirichlet character is induced. A primitive character has conductor equal to its modulus. Read in context
Covolume
The volume of a fundamental parallelepiped of a lattice in its real span with the chosen metric. Read in context
Critical strip / σ
The region 0 < Re s < 1. Here s = σ+it; σ = ½ is the critical line. Read in context
Cyclotomic layer
A stage in a tower formed by adjoining roots of unity. In the ℓ-adic tower here, layer m has relative degree ℓᵐ over the chosen base field. Read in context
Discrete valuation ring
A ring in which each nonzero element has the form uπⁿ for a unit u, a fixed uniformizer π and a nonnegative integer n. This exponent measures divisibility. For ℤℓ one can take π=ℓ. Read in context
Doubling integral
An integral obtained by placing two copies of a unitary group in a larger group and pairing a section there with matrix coefficients on the original copies. Its local factors determine the partial L-function in the theta norm. Read in context
Elementary divisors
A pair of full local lattices can be put into diagonal form by integral changes of bases. The integer exponents of a uniformizer classify their relative position. Their sum gives the determinant valuation and the sum of their absolute values gives the degree in the split-place series. Read in context
Euler’s constant / γ
The limit of 1+1/2+⋯+1/n − log n; it is the constant Laurent coefficient of ζ at 1. Read in context
Formal group and formal logarithm
A group law in power series at the identity and a change of coordinate taking that law to addition. Read in context
Frobenius and inertia
Inertia is the subgroup of a local Galois group acting trivially on the residue field. A Frobenius class acts there by raising to the residue-field size. Read in context
Haar measure
A translation-invariant measure on a locally compact group. Its normalization matters when comparing the two sides of the Petersson identity. Read in context
Hecke operator
An operator formed by summing translates indexed by the cosets in a double coset. The operator in the invariant-period comparison divides by the number of cosets, so it fixes constants. Read in context
Homology and cohomology
A cycle is a chain with zero boundary, and its homology class records it up to addition of boundaries. A cohomology class tests cycles by a cocycle, which vanishes on boundaries. Torsion counts and bounds on the dimension over the reals give different information. Read in context
Jacobi sum
For two multiplicative characters of a finite field, extended by zero at zero, sum χ(x)φ(1−x) over the field. In the nondegenerate cyclotomic case the product with the inverse-character sum is the size of the field. Read in context
Matrix coefficient
The scalar formed by translating one vector by a group element and pairing it with a second vector. When the vectors coincide, its value at the identity is their squared norm. Read in context
Mellin transform
Here the integral from 1 to infinity against x^(−s−1) dx. Under x=eᵘ it becomes a Laplace transform. Read in context
Module length
Number of simple quotients in a composition series. For a finite ℤℓ-module of order ℓᵈ the length is d. Its minimum number of generators can be smaller. Read in context
Multiplicity / m
At an isolated zero ρ, the integer m≥1 such that L(s)=(s−ρ)ᵐh(s) with h holomorphic and h(ρ)≠0. The balance chapter also uses m for a chain length. Read in context
Period of an automorphic form
An integral of an automorphic function over a specified quotient, often after restricting the function to a subgroup or pairing it with a second function. The bottom period here restricts a core function to the row group and integrates their product. Read in context
Petersson pairing
The integral of the product of an automorphic function and the complex conjugate of another on the automorphic quotient, with the chosen measure. On the diagonal it gives a squared norm. Read in context
Poincaré series
A sum of translates of a test function over a discrete group or a coset space. The sum used to separate a row has an identity contribution large enough to dominate the sum of the other contributions after unfolding its period. Read in context
Pole and residue
The residue is the coefficient of (s−ρ)⁻¹ in a Laurent expansion. A simple pole has a nonzero residue and no higher-order singular terms. Read in context
Positive part / u₊
The number max(u,0). In a ramp it cuts off the sum; in the window estimate it keeps the side controlled by the hypothesis. Read in context
Ramification index
The multiplicity of a prime in the extended prime ideal. It enters the weighting of the finite-place contributions to divisibility. Read in context
Residue degree
The degree of an extension of residue fields. In an unramified quadratic extension, a split base prime has two primes of residue degree one. An inert prime has one of degree two. Read in context
Satake parameters
The unramified eigenvalue data that encode the action of the spherical Hecke operators at one place. The normalization used here specifies their powers of the residue-field size in the local Euler factors. Read in context
Saturation of a lattice
The saturation of Λ in E is the set of x in E such that kx lies in Λ for some nonzero integer k. Modulo Λ it gives exactly the torsion subgroup of E/Λ. Read in context
Semisimple representation
A direct sum of irreducible representations. One can compare multiplicities constituent by constituent once an isomorphism of the global representations has been established. Read in context
Sha-type local kernel
The subgroup of degree-one Galois cohomology classes whose restrictions at the specified places vanish. Its module length enters the cohomological window bound. Read in context
Shapiro’s isomorphism
Homology with an induced coefficient module can be computed in the subgroup. Here the module of functions on a finite cyclic quotient turns a homology calculation for G into one for the kernel. Read in context
Slope
For a Euclidean lattice of positive rank, its Arakelov slope is the negative log of its covolume divided by its rank. Extremal slopes measure sublattices and quotients. Read in context
Spherical data
At a good finite place the vectors are fixed by the specified maximal compact subgroup. This invariance permits the local integral to be evaluated by Hecke eigenvalues. Read in context
Stabilizer
The subgroup of symmetries that take an object to itself. In the arithmetic face count a finite stabilizer acts faithfully on a finite set of shortest vectors. Its order contributes to the bound for integral homology. Read in context
Stickelberger element
An element of a group ring acting on ideal classes in cyclotomic arithmetic. Its annihilation property supplies a relation on a quotient of the class group in the tower argument. Read in context
Tate twist
A change in a Galois action by a power of the cyclotomic character, which records the action on roots of unity. Its exponent matters in a local restriction kernel. Read in context
Theta lift
An integral operator with a theta kernel carrying an automorphic function between spaces for two groups. Read in context
Trace-dual basis
For a finite separable extension, bases (uᵢ) and (vⱼ) are trace-dual when Tr(uᵢvⱼ) is 1 for i=j and 0 otherwise. Then x=∑ᵢ Tr(xuᵢ)vᵢ. Clearing both bases at once produces the split residue calculation. Read in context
Valuation / vₚ
The exponent of p in a nonzero rational number, extended by vₚ(0)=+∞. The normalized p-adic absolute value is p to the power −vₚ. Read in context
Von Mangoldt function / Λ
Λ(pʲ)=log p for a prime p and integer j≥1; its value at every other positive integer is zero. Read in context
Window / Y, U, δ
An interval [Y,Y¹⁺ᵟ] with δ>0; in logarithmic coordinates its endpoints are U=log Y and (1+δ)U. Read in context
Witt index
Largest dimension of a subspace on which the entire Hermitian pairing vanishes. It determines which convergence hypothesis is required for the doubled theta integral. Read in context

3The critical strip, in section

The frontispiece plots |ζ(σ+it)| on 27 equally spaced sections 0≤σ≤1. Each section contains 169 samples, 8≤t≤32. The copper section on this page has σ=½. The opening figure allows other sampled sections to be selected. Height is linear in |ζ|. Smoothly joined sample values can miss a zero between mesh points.

The generator sums the first 95 Dirichlet terms, then applies Euler–Maclaurin at N=96 through B₆. The absolute remainder is bounded by |s(s+1)⋯(s+5)| N−σ−5/[30240(σ+5)] from the periodic Bernoulli remainder. This is less than 0.000002 across the plotted domain.
