Where does the exponent come from? For the indicator of the original lattice the weighted difference between the averaged theta function and the Eisenstein series is bounded after reduction to a Siegel domain. The dilation replaces the real upper-half-plane variable x+i by (x+i)/a². Its imaginary part is a−2 at each of the d real places. The product of the local weights (Im τ)M/2 is therefore a−dM. For E = ℚ(i), F = ℚ, J = I₃ and β = 1, normalize the standard coordinate lattices to volume one. Modulo 3 each of three complex coordinates contributes a sum of two squares. Counting the 729 vectors gives 252 of norm 1 and 225 of norm 0. Of the latter, 224 are nonzero. Each nonzero solution has one independent scalar Gram condition on its six next digits, giving 3⁵ lifts for each prescribed next target digit. Thus primitive vectors contribute 224/3⁵ at target 9.
