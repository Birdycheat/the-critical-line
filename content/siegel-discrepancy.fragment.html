Check the two height powers in dimension three

Take F = ℚ, E = ℚ(i), M = 3 and a single height Y = 4. The innermost ball has radius 1/2. Its two powers in the count are (1/2)³ and (1/2)⁶. The theta weight is 43/2 = 8, so their weighted sum is 9/8, before the fixed counting constant. At height 16 the same computation gives 65/64.
