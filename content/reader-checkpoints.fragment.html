What does one arithmetic measurement buy?

Fix a level, a scale x in its arithmetic window and the sampling data at that scale. At translation σ, subtract the predicted main term from the prime-power count and sum over the samples and chain stages. Call this real difference Δσ. The lattice argument gives an upper bound Err for each Δσ. The character calculation reads the same differences in the opposite direction. Write B for the weighted sum of the retained real parts after subtracting the allowance for discarded characters.

Set X = x½+ε and write Q for the total weight. It is the product of the number of samples, the number of stages and the weight per measurement. The average runs over the translation subgroup. At each translation the calculation keeps v of the 2n weight positions, a fraction v/(2n).
