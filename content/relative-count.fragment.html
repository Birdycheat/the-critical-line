Which parameters enter the dimension comparison?

Fix a positive level-growth exponent Cg. The theorem chooses a lower bound for the ambient dimension n before varying the field and level. At each of the f infinite places the discrete series has the prescribed signature (p, q), with p + q = n and q² ≤ n. The CM field has root discriminant d with log d ≤ exp(n), and log f ≤ √n. The scale b ≥ 2 satisfies log b ≤ exp(nf log n/16). The congruence level g lies between 1 and bCg. The auxiliary rational prime exceeds 4n and splits completely in the CM field.

The absolute values of the archimedean weight entries are ≤ b². Whenever two entries at the same place have opposite signature signs, their distance is at least 8·10¹⁴(Cg + 4)n¹⁹f⁵ log(bd). This gap supplies rapid decay for the far terms. The threshold for a point to lie in the deep cusp uses the same field and level data and equals τ∗ = (nf d g b)−11q.

What does a short sublattice measure?

Let Λ be the lattice at the point and let B be a nonzero sublattice of rank s over the CM field E. Embed B at the f conjugate pairs using the positive metrics at that point. Its real covolume is taken in its own span, of real dimension 2fs. Multiply the covolume by 2fs|disc E|−s/2 and take the (2fs)th root. This is its normalized size. The least size is the infimum over all such B. The root makes it meaningful to compare different ranks. The canonical lattice filtration has a nonzero step strictly below t precisely when the least size is < t. Its step at or below t is already nonzero at equality.

For example, give the two coordinate directions of ℤ[i]² lengths ½ and 2, respectively. Here f = 1 and |disc E| = 4, so the prefactor is one in every rank. The fundamental square of the first Gaussian line has area ¼ and its normalized size is ½. The full lattice has four-dimensional covolume one. To find the least size, project a line sublattice onto a nonzero coordinate. This identifies it with a Gaussian ideal. An element of least nonzero norm generates that ideal. Round the real and imaginary parts of each quotient to integers. The remainder has smaller norm than the divisor and belongs to the ideal, so it must be zero. Thus the line has a generator (a,b) with squared length |a|²/4 + 4|b|² ≥ ¼. Every full-rank sublattice has positive integer index, hence covolume at least one. The least size is exactly ½. The strict test used in the short-flag bound therefore excludes this example at the boundary.

The bound still contains the unknown D on the right. Write X = |D − M|. Since D ≤ M + X, we get (1 − δ)X ≤ (2α + 2δ)M. The threshold on n ensures n ≥ exp(15).
