Which cochains detect the translations in the upper-right block?

A translation in the affine block is a matrix in the additive group A = Matn×r(𝒪L′). Its entries lie in a free abelian group of finite rank. We first work with any such A and trivial real coefficients. A homogeneous cochain of degree j is a function on Aj+1 unchanged when the same element is added to all its vertices. The coboundary δ sums the values obtained by omitting vertex i, with sign (−1)i starting at i = 0. Tuples can have repeated vertices. A unit square distinguishes the two orders

Take the coordinate characters x, y on ℤ², with unit vectors u, v in that order. The bar simplex [a|b] has vertices 0, a, a + b. Its boundary is [b] − [a + b] + [a]. Thus z = [u|v] − [v|u] is a cycle. On its two triangles x ∪ y has successive values 1 and 0, so it evaluates to 1 on z. Reversing the characters gives 0 and 1, hence −1 on z. Every coboundary evaluates to zero on this cycle, so the first cup represents a nonzero class.

In the affine argument a separate eigenvalue calculation supplies a polynomial Fj that kills each cup word under pullback by multiplication by a chosen unit v. Applying it to c leaves δ(Fj(v∗)e), because pullback commutes with δ and preserves invariance. This is where generation gives the annihilator on every class. Generation holds in all positive degrees.
