Which logarithm admits the same approximation for every Galois element?

Two systems of roots occur in the local period rings. The variable X of Bst = Bcris[X] comes from compatible ℓ-power roots of ℓ. The parameter u of the Breuil–Kisin ring 𝔖 = W(kK)[[u]] comes from compatible roots of a uniformizer π of the finite extension K/ℚℓ. Let E be its monic Eisenstein polynomial and e its degree. We choose a logarithm in the first ring whose Galois translation can be computed to any prescribed filtration depth using the second ring.

The two root systems. Choose compatible primitive ℓnth roots of unity ζn, and put t = log([ε]) for the corresponding cyclotomic period. Write πn for the chosen ℓnth root of π. For each g in GK the ratio g(πn)/πn is a power of ζn. Raising the ratio at index n + 1 to the ℓth power gives the ratio at index n. The exponents therefore define one ℓ-adic integer cπ(g). For the roots of ℓ the same construction defines κℓ on the full absolute Galois group of ℚℓ. We restrict it to GK, where it can be compared with cπ using the same roots of unity.

In the tilt, denote the two root systems by π♭ and ℓ♭, and write [·] for the Teichmüller lift. The Eisenstein equation gives |π|e = |ℓ|. Indeed |π| < 1 and the nonconstant terms below degree e have smaller absolute value than the constant term. Hence x = (π♭)e/ℓ♭ is a unit of the valuation ring of the tilt. Its Galois exponent at every index is e cπ(g) − κℓ(g), by taking the quotient of the two root ratios.

The crystalline logarithm of this unit gives b ∈ Bcris. More explicitly, split off its residue-field Teichmüller factor and take the logarithm of the principal-unit factor. The integral construction takes an ℓ-power of the principal unit first so its logarithm lies in Acris, then divides the logarithm by the same power of ℓ. The residue factor is fixed under the Galois translations in question, since the quotient of the conjugate by the original unit has untilt 1. Equivariance and additivity of the logarithm now give g(b) − b = (e cπ(g) − κℓ(g))t. Frobenius raises Teichmüller lifts to their ℓth powers, so φ(b) = ℓb. The convergence and equivariance of the principal-unit logarithm are the period-ring inputs here. Brinon and Conrad, §9.2, Lemma 9.2.2.

L = X + b,   c(g) = e cπ(g),   g(L) = L + c(g)t,   φ(L) = ℓL.

Indeed g(X) − X = κℓ(g)t and φ(X) = ℓX. The two appearances of κℓ cancel. In particular c(g) = 0 whenever g fixes the entire uniformizer root tower. This gives the translation used in the specialization at logarithm zero.

One scalar for the whole group. Let ι evaluate 𝔖 in Ainf at u = [π♭], followed by the map into BdR+. For each integer m ≥ 0 there are an integer km ≥ 0 and a single sm ∈ E𝔖 such that

g(ι(sm)) − ι(sm) ≡ ℓkmc(g)t   modulo FilmBdR+,   g ∈ GK.

At each depth the construction chooses sm before applying any g. Its denominator can depend on the depth. For example, take K = ℚℓ, π = ℓ, the same root tower on both sides, and E(u) = u − ℓ. At depth two choose s₂ = E and k₂ = 1. The ratio g([π♭])/[π♭] has logarithm κℓ(g)t. Modulo Fil² its difference from 1 equals this logarithm, since the square of Fil¹ vanishes there. Also [π♭] reduces to ℓ modulo Fil¹. Multiplying gives g(ι(E)) − ι(E) ≡ ℓκℓ(g)t, as required.

Construction of the scalar by a finite logarithm

At depth zero take s₀ = 0. For m ≥ 1 work in T = 𝔖[1/ℓ]/(E(u)m) and write J = (E(u)) in this ring. The quotient T/J is K, with u mapping to π, by the chosen integral presentation 𝔖/(E) ≃ 𝒪K. There is a unique root v of E in T lifting π. Indeed E′(π) ≠ 0 by separability in characteristic zero. Starting from u, subtract E(v₀)/E′(v₀) from the current lift v₀. The derivative is a unit because its reduction is a unit and J is nilpotent. Polynomial Taylor expansion squares the order of the error at each step, so the process terminates. For uniqueness, the difference of the values of E at two such lifts is their difference times a factor reducing to E′(π), again a unit.

Since π ≠ 0 in K, its lift v is also invertible in T. Thus h = u/v − 1 belongs to J. Form the finite sum H = e ∑1≤j<m(−1)j−1hj/j in J. Choose a representative E(u)w of H in 𝔖[1/ℓ]. By the definition of localization some single power ℓkm clears w in 𝔖. Put sm = ℓkmE(u)w. This uses a localization of the whole power-series ring, so one exponent clears the full series.

Evaluate in Rm = BdR+/Film. Both ι and g ∘ ι factor through T, since E(π) = 0 and g fixes π. The two images of v are roots of the same polynomial E with the same residue π. Its coefficients lie in W(kK) and are fixed by GK. The same uniqueness argument, now for the nilpotent ideal Fil¹/Film, proves that the two images agree. Consequently the quotient of the two images of u/v is exactly the image of the Teichmüller lift of g(π♭)/π♭. Its logarithm is cπ(g)t, by the cyclotomic logarithm identity log([εa]) = a t for a ∈ ℤℓ. In a nilpotent ideal of a rational algebra the finite logarithm takes products to sums, by the formal power-series identity reduced modulo total degree m. Thus the difference of the two values of H is e cπ(g)t. Multiplying by the chosen power of ℓ proves the scalar congruence for every g.
