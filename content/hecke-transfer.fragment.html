Which cosets carry the original period into the next Hecke average?

Fix the smaller size n while enlarging to N = n + r. To obtain the lower bound on a period, we need the share of the Hecke sum that acts as the original period on a stabilized cycle. That share has a concrete description in the residue field.

Write ΓN = GLN(𝒪L′), q = diag(π,1,…,1) and k = 𝒪L′/(π). A matrix γ lies in the subgroup SN that labels the Hecke cosets precisely when qγq−1 is integral and invertible over the integers. Conjugation divides the first column below its top entry by π and multiplies the rest of the first row by π. The determinant stays a unit. Thus membership is equivalent to preserving the line ke₁ modulo π. For a right coset SNc, take the residue line of c−1e₁. Two representatives determine the same line exactly when they determine the same coset. There are finitely many such lines, and swapping the first coordinate with each coordinate realizes N distinct lines. In particular the index IN is at least N. Why summing the other cosets recovers an unshifted period

The smaller group permutes the complementary set X of cosets by right multiplication. For each x take the difference Φx between the cocycle translated by its Hecke representative and the original stabilized cocycle. On cycles of the stabilizer of x, this difference has zero period. Here the nonzero coordinate in the last block matters. It permits a change of basis in which the translated stabilizer acts by integral matrices with diagonal blocks h and 1 and only one possible off-diagonal block above them. The arithmetic affine-block homology comparison identifies the image of a stabilizer cycle under this action with its block-diagonal image. To pass from a stabilizer to the whole group, choose one representative x₀ per orbit. The cochain criterion for a zero class supplies a primitive Ψ on tuples from the whole smaller group, invariant under the stabilizer, with δΨ = Φx₀. Transport it to each x in that orbit by an element carrying x₀ to x. Two such choices differ on the left by a stabilizer element, so give the same translate.
