Why does an index computed on the generic fibre still count lifts after reduction?

For good reduction we need the degree of a source map over the entire local model. For the rest of the good-reduction construction, write Y for the whole integral fibre power previously denoted by 𝒵, and Y′ for its analogue at a finer level. Let s ∶ Y′ → Y forget the finer marking. The groups of equivalent markings are K₁ ⊆ K₀. At this stage the moduli comparison for forgetting level gives a finite étale map on the integral bases and a Cartesian square of abelian schemes. Passing to their fibre powers is another base change, so s is finite étale everywhere. Its degree still has to be determined.

Integral degree lemma. Assume that a common fine level covers the two generic fibres, that its maps identify precisely the orbits of K₁ and K₀ respectively, and that the stabilizer of a fine lift is its principal level subgroup, contained in K₁. Assume also that the base Y is flat over the valuation ring. Then the number of points in every geometric fibre of s is

#s−1(y) = [K₀ : K₁].

Generic fibre. Choose a geometric point y over the fraction field and a fine lift x. For u ∈ K₀, project Tu−1(x) = x · u to Y′, using the right action. Each point above y is obtained this way, since it lifts to the common fine level and all lifts above y lie in one K₀-orbit. Two choices u, v give the same point when their lifts lie in the same K₁-orbit. The fine-level subgroup is normal in K₀ and lies in K₁, so the stabilizer introduces no further identifications. Equivalently u−1v ∈ K₁. These are precisely the cosets counted by the index. Here surjectivity onto the generic fibres of these particular quotient models comes from the integral level comparison.

Special fibre. Since s is finite étale, its algebra is locally a free module of finite rank. The rank is locally constant on Y. At a geometric point the fibre algebra is a product of copies of its algebraically closed field, one for each point. Its rank is therefore the number we just computed on the generic fibre. Stacks Project, §29.49 and Lemma 58.2.2.

Take any point of the special fibre and an open neighbourhood on which the rank is constant. Going down for the flat structure map lifts the generic point of the valuation ring to a generalization of the chosen point. Every open neighbourhood of the chosen point contains this generalization. The generic computation gives the value of the rank there, hence also at the original point. This proves the degree formula at every geometric point of the special fibre. Stacks Project, Lemma 10.39.19.

The étale hypothesis is used in the equality between rank and point count. For comparison, over ℤ localized at 5 the algebra with equation X(X − 5) = 0 has module rank two everywhere. At 5 its fibre algebra is 𝔽₅[X]/(X²), supported at one point even after algebraic closure. The two integral models at 5 show this collision.
