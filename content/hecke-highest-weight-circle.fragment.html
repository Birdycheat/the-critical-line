Which powers can occur when a weight is restricted to one circle?

The family of forms gives functions of a complex number z on the unit circle. To read off a weight from these functions we need to know which powers of z a representation can contribute. For 0 ≤ k ≤ a, let tk(z) be the diagonal matrix with z in the first k positions and 1 elsewhere. Let κ₁ ≥ ⋯ ≥ κa be integers and let sk(κ) = κ₁ + ⋯ + κk, with s₀ = 0. Write Vκ for the irreducible algebraic representation of GLa(ℂ) with this highest weight.

Circle coefficient bound. For any vector w in this representation and any complex linear functional L, the function z ↦ L(tk(z)w) is a finite Laurent sum. Every exponent m with nonzero coefficient satisfies

kκa ≤ m ≤ sk(κ).

The upper bound is attained on the highest-weight vector with any functional that is nonzero there. For the fixed initial weight this supplies a bound in both directions. Changing the embedding to its complex conjugate replaces z by its inverse and reverses the exponents.

Proof. Put b = κa and mj = κj − κj+1 for 1 ≤ j < a. The highest-weight construction realizes Vκ inside a tensor product. Take mj copies of the jth exterior power of ℂa and multiply the action by the determinant to power b. In each exterior factor choose the vector e₁ ∧ ⋯ ∧ ej of the standard basis. Their tensor generates the required irreducible representation. The determinant factor permits negative entries in κ. P. Etingof, Representations of GLₙ, I, Proposition 27.1 and §§27.2–27.3.

A basis vector of the jth exterior power selects j distinct indices. Its power of z counts how many of them lie among the first k. This is between 0 and min(j, k). Exponents add under tensor products, and the determinant contributes kb. The largest possible sum is kb + ∑j min(j, k)mj. To evaluate it, count one copy of mj for each index i ≤ k with i ≤ j. For each i the inner sum telescopes to κi − b. This gives exactly sk(κ). Every term in an expansion in the tensor basis lies in the claimed interval, so taking linear combinations and evaluating L preserves it. The chosen highest tensor selects precisely the first j indices in each factor and attains every maximum simultaneously.

An invertible upper triangular matrix preserves the span of the first j basis vectors. On their top exterior product it acts by the product of its first j diagonal entries. Multiplying over the same tensor factors shows that on the highest vector its scalar is ∏i biiκi for an upper triangular matrix (bij). This gives the triangular transformation used in the weight family.

In rank two take κ = (2, −1). The representation is the third symmetric power of ℂ² twisted by the inverse determinant. The monomial with j copies of e₁ and 3 − j copies of e₂ has exponent j − 1 at t₁. Only the highest monomial attains 2. At t₂ both coordinates scale, and all four monomials have exponent 1. The highest weight specifies the largest exponent available in the representation. A chosen vector may have a smaller one.
