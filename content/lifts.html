What is being lifted?

A Hermitian form measures lengths using both a field and its conjugation. On two complex coordinates the example is ⟨x,y⟩ = x₁y₁ + x₂y₂. Its unitary group consists of the changes of coordinates preserving this pairing. For a number field we can make such a change at each place. An adelic group element collects these choices, integral at almost every finite place. A rational change of coordinates gives the same change simultaneously in all completions.

An automorphic form here is a function on the adelic group, invariant under those rational changes on the left and satisfying additional finiteness and regularity conditions. Translating its argument on the right produces a space of functions carrying a group action. The irreducible space being lifted is U, and φ is one vector in it. A theta kernel converts φ into a function on another group by integration. To show that this conversion retains some information, one proves that a resulting vector has positive squared norm. At a good finite prime the commuting Hecke operators then provide a list of spectral parameters, with multiplicities, which can be compared on the two sides.
