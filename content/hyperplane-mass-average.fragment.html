Which rational hyperplanes share the determinant budget?

Fix a positive integer d. We want the sum of the lattice masses on rational hyperplanes that admit an integral full lattice of discriminant order d². The ambient Hermitian space is totally positive definite over a CM field E, unramified at finite places over its real field F. Write R = 𝒪E, D = [E ∶ ℚ] = 2f, λ = log₂ D, and j ≥ 1 for the hyperplane dimension. A rational hyperplane is counted up to the E-linear isometries of the ambient space. Its lattices are counted up to isometry within the hyperplane, with weight 1/|Aut(P)|. In the row count the chosen lattice will be the intersection with an ambient lattice. Here we sum over all qualifying lattice classes inside each hyperplane.

Take an integral lattice P of the required order in a hyperplane U and enlarge it to a maximal integral lattice A in U. Let S be its set of bad prime ideals of R. At a bad prime 𝔓 the dual quotient has one residue line. If q𝔓 is the residue size of the underlying place of F, the order of that line is q𝔓². Let m be the product of these q𝔓 and i the additive index of P in A. The local classification and the index-square identity give the first two equalities below. The set S determines the rational isometry class of U at this rank by the classification and extension theorem. Choose one representative for each realized set. Several sets may have the same product m. To count them, fix a rational prime p dividing m > 1. Some q𝔓 in the product is divisible by p, so 𝔓 lies above p. There are at most D choices for 𝔓 by the prime decomposition formula. Removing it leaves product m/q𝔓 ≤ m/2. At m = 1 only the empty set occurs. Induction bounds the number at m by D(m/2)λ = mλ. How much mass can one set of bad primes carry?

For one representative U let μU(d²) be the sum of 1/|Aut(P)| over integral lattice classes of discriminant order d² in U. Write Mj for the genus mass of the standard orthonormal lattice of rank j. All maximal lattices in U lie in one genus. The maximal-lattice mass bound gives that genus mass at most Mj(m²)j/2 = Mjmj. For each maximal lattice the number of sublattices of index i is at most ij+λ. Marking a maximal enlargement counts each smaller lattice with its automorphism weight at least once. Why can the masses be summed without another orbit factor?

Fix a divisor m of d. At most mλ sets of bad primes contribute, and each contributes at most B/mλ. Their total at this one divisor is at most B. There are at most d positive divisors. The empty set at m = 1 already accounts for every admissible rational class with a unimodular maximal lattice at this rank. Taking one representative of each realized orbit gives

Σ[U] μU(d²) ≤ ∑m∣d B ≤ dB = Mjdj+2+log₂f.

The last exponent uses λ = log₂(2f) = 1 + log₂ f. The saving comes from the index d/m in the fixed-index count. Replacing that index by d before summing would discard the denominator that pays for the possible sets of bad primes.

Return to the row at its completely split level prime T, with t = Tf. Marking a hyperplane in an ambient lattice counts each pair with weight the reciprocal of its stabilizer order. Once its rational hyperplane is fixed, the perpendicular is a line. Its integral intersection has discriminant order at most (dt)². The summed line mass is therefore at most M₁(dt)2+log₂f, uniformly in the chosen hyperplane. For any fixed pair of intersection lattices at most t²d² gluings give an ambient lattice.
