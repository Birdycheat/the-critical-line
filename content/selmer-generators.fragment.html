Counting the generators before bounding the length

Fix an odd prime ℓ dividing the degree of the field E. The determinant calculation above tells us how many powers of ℓ annihilate the minus group. One also needs to bound how many independent classes can survive a single multiplication by ℓ. Write X for the ambient twisted first cohomology group and X[ℓ] for its subgroup killed by ℓ. The argument counts this subgroup even though it only needs a bound for the minus classes.

Let F = E(μℓ) and put m = [F:ℚ], n = [E:ℚ]. The source chooses, for each class in X[ℓ], a cocycle taking values in the order-ℓ subgroup of ℚℓ/ℤℓ. Indeed, if ℓ times a representative is the coboundary of a coefficient b, choose b′ with ℓb′ = b in the divisible group ℚℓ/ℤℓ. Subtract the coboundary of b′. The new representative is killed by ℓ pointwise. Over F the cyclotomic character is 1 modulo ℓ. Restricted to this subgroup of values, the twisted cocycle rule becomes ordinary addition. One obtains an additive character of the absolute Galois group of F, unramified outside the primes over ℓ because the original cocycle factors through the Galois group with that ramification condition. If two chosen cocycles give the same character, their difference restricts to zero. The index [F:E] divides ℓ − 1, so averaging over the cosets is allowed in ℤℓ. The coboundary lemma then identifies their classes over E. This gives an injection of sets. If E embeds in the cyclotomic field of level N, then F embeds at level ℓN. To count this quotient, remove the primes in S from the fractional ideal of a and divide the remaining prime exponents by ℓ. Its class in the ideal class group with these primes inverted belongs to ClS(F)[ℓ]. Every such class occurs because its ℓth power is principal away from S, and a generator gives an admissible a. The kernel consists of S-units modulo powers. These are the elements with valuation zero outside S. For a concrete count, take ℚ, S = {3} and cubes. Over F, the S-unit factor has order ℓr₁+r₂−1+|S| |μℓ(F)|, where r₁ counts real embeddings and r₂ counts conjugate pairs. The root-of-unity factor is at most ℓ, both r₁+r₂ and |S| are at most m, and the order of ClS(F)[ℓ] is at most the ordinary class number hF.
