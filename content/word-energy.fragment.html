Each term t contributes a vector vt supported on one pair of lattices. Its row records those lattices and a finite index i. For either group S, form ai by summing its vectors with index i. In the square of that sum, terms with the same index but different lattice pairs have disjoint support. Absolute convergence permits regrouping, so only ordered pairs in the same row contribute. The large-corank estimate bounds the sum of absolute inner products over all same-row pairs of nonzero-label terms of corank at least two by εΔ, including the factor Γ₁. Thus each group can use this bound on its own subset. In G, the nonscalar corank-one pairs cost at most another εΔ in absolute value. The signed sum of the scalar pairs is at most AΔ. In N, one signed bound ε′Δ covers all corank-one pairs. These are the only cases, giving

Φ(G) ≤ (A + 2ε)Δ,   Φ(N) ≤ (ε + ε′)Δ.

The common Gram matrix converts each energy to an upper bound on the squared Hilbert–Schmidt norm of its operator sum, with a factor P. The triangle inequality then gives the retained block bound

‖K₀‖² ≤ (√[P(A + 2ε)] + √[P(ε + ε′)])² Δ ≤ (1 + b−6)Δ.
